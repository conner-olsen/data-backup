Privacy policy

Google Drive files are accessed, stored, modified or deleted only as directed by the account owner. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared publicly by this application. Credentials are retained on the account owner's computer; files remain in locations selected by the owner.

The account owner controls retention and may revoke access through Google Account settings. Revoking access does not itself delete existing files. This page uses no cookies or analytics and contains no account data.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
